Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

History Domain Cleaner ("the extension") is provided by Hüseyin Örer ("we"). This policy covers the extension and the website historycleaner.app. The extension works entirely on your device: we do not collect, receive, sell or share your browsing history or any other personal data from the extension.

What the extension does with your data

The extension reads your browser history only to show and delete the entries you ask for, to suggest site names you have visited, and to run automatic rules you create. Deleted entries are removed from your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

It saves these preferences locally on your device (chrome.storage): up to 8 recently used site names, your last selected time range, your "out-of-range visits" preference, and the automatic rules you create (site name, rule type, number of days).

Files you create with the "Export" feature are saved only to your own device, at your request.

Permissions

"history": read and delete history entries you choose; suggest visited sites; run your rules.

"storage": save the preferences, rules, vault and license described in this policy on your device.

"activeTab": read the address of the current tab when you open the extension, so that site can be pre-selected.

"favicon": show website icons from the browser's local icon cache.

"contextMenus": add "Clear this site's history" to the right-click menu.

"alarms": run your automatic rules on a schedule.

Encrypted vault (Pro)

If you use the encrypted vault, the entries you move into it (page address, title and visit time) are encrypted on your device before they are saved, using AES-256-GCM with a key protected by your vault password (PBKDF2-SHA256). The vault is stored only on your device. We never receive your password or vault contents and cannot recover them: if you forget your password, the vault cannot be opened. Uninstalling the extension or resetting the vault permanently deletes it.

Pro license and trial

If you activate Pro, your license key is stored locally on your device and verified offline; the extension does not contact any server to check it. The license key contains an order reference and a date, and no personal information.

If you start the free Pro trial, the trial start date (a single timestamp) is saved with chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Google syncs this value between your signed-in browsers, as it does for other browser settings. No other data is stored in sync storage.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects the information needed to complete your order (such as your name, email address, billing country and payment details) and processes it under its own privacy policy (paddle.com/legal/privacy). We receive your email address and order details from Paddle and use them only to deliver your license key, provide support and meet legal and tax obligations.

Website

The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses in server logs to deliver and secure the website.

Children

The extension and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights and contact

You can delete all data stored by the extension at any time by uninstalling it. For questions about this policy or to exercise your data protection rights regarding purchase information, contact support@historycleaner.app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version; material changes will also be noted in the extension's store listing.
