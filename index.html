Questions

Is my browsing history sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. The extension has no server, no analytics and no account. Even Pro licenses are checked offline.

Why does it say a page was “also visited outside the range”?

Chrome can only delete a page together with all of its visits. If you visited a page today and last week, deleting “today” would also remove last week’s visit. The extension tells you and lets you skip those pages.

What happens if I forget my vault password?

The vault is encrypted with your password and we never see it, so it cannot be recovered. You can reset the vault, which permanently deletes its contents.

How do I get Pro?

Start the free trial or buy Pro from the Pro page inside the extension. After purchase you receive a license key by email; paste it into the extension to activate.

Can I get a refund?

Which browsers are supported?
